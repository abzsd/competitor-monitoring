Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrade instantly — we'll prorate the difference. Downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens after the free trial?

Your 14-day trial includes full Professional features. No credit card required. After the trial, pick any plan or your account pauses (data kept for 90 days).

Do you offer non-profit or educational discounts?

Yes — 40% off any plan for verified non-profits, NGOs, and educational institutions. Contact sales to apply.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We're SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Enterprise plans add on-premise deployment and data residency options.
