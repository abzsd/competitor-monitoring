Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. Upgrade instantly — we'll prorate the difference. Downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens after the free trial?

Your 7-day trial includes full Professional features including RivalAI Pro. Credit card required to start. After the trial, pick any plan or cancel with no charge.

What is RivalAI and is it included?

RivalAI is our built-in AI assistant that auto-generates status reports, predicts sprint risks, and answers questions about your projects. Basic access (50 queries/mo) is included in Starter. Pro and Enterprise get unlimited access with advanced features like predictive estimates and smart summaries.

Do you offer non-profit or educational discounts?

Yes — 40% off any plan for verified non-profits, NGOs, and educational institutions. Contact sales to apply.

Is my data secure?

All data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). We hold SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and are GDPR compliant. Enterprise plans add HIPAA compliance, on-premise deployment, and data residency in US, EU, or APAC regions.
