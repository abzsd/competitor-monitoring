Product Launch

Launching RivalAI: AI That Actually Understands Your Projects

Today we're launching RivalAI, our built-in AI assistant that goes beyond simple automation. RivalAI can predict project risks, auto-generate status reports, answer complex questions about your work, auto-triage bugs, and suggest sprint plans — all powered by our proprietary model trained on millions of project management workflows.

Key capabilities: Smart status summaries (save 5+ hrs/week), predictive sprint planning (90% accuracy on capacity estimates), risk detection (caught 73% of blockers in beta before they became critical), and natural language project queries. Available in Professional and Enterprise plans.

What makes it different: Unlike bolt-on AI features from competitors, RivalAI is deeply integrated into every workflow. It reads your task history, understands team dynamics, and learns your project patterns. And your data never leaves your tenant — we built our own model specifically for this.
